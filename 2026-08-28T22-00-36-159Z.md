## Deutsche Bank selects Thought Machine for major core systems overhaul

Deutsche Bank in Germany is updating the infrastructure behind its Private Bank division with the Vault Core system developed by UK technology company Thought Machine.

Vault Core will be used under a 10-year agreement to support all banking and lending products in Germany for both personal banking and wealth management.

The move to Thought Machine aligns with a three-year modernisation strategy aiming to reduce the number of core banking systems it uses from 15 implementations to two, with Vault Core being the first selection.

Deutsche Bank is investing around €600 million into its Private Bank systems consolidation, expecting annual run-rate savings of €300 million by 2028.

## Vanguard inks $4bn deal to acquire digital custodian Altruist

Vanguard has agreed a deal to acquire Altruist, a digital wealth management platform and custodian for independent financial advisors. The agreement values Altruist at approximately $4 billion, The Wall Street Journal reports.

Launched by founder and CEO Jason Wenk in 2018, Altruist serves over 6,000 financial advisors through its self-clearing brokerage, providing solutions for fee billing, portfolio management and reporting, and account opening.

Vanguard has been supporting Altruist for the last six years, adding to a Series A extension in 2020 before investing in the company through its $50 million Series B round.

Vanguard expects to close the deal later this year. Altruist will continue operating as a standalone business, retaining its current operating model, branding, and leadership.

## US SEC proposes new framework for crypto assets

The US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has proposed new crypto guidelines designed to create a "clear and fit-for-purpose framework for certain investment contracts involving crypto assets", states the regulator.

Under the new rules, two exemptions from the registration requirements of the Securities Act of 1933 would apply to "certain investment contracts involving crypto assets", says the regulator.

The first is a one-time exemption that would allow firms to issue up to $5 million in crypto tokens during a four-year period, while the second would permit offerings of up to $75 million every 12 months.

The proposals are aimed at addressing "long-standing barriers to responsible capital formation and innovation within domestic crypto asset markets, while preserving the investor protections at the core of federal securities laws", adds the SEC.

## Lunar founders launch new AI audit company Repodo

The founders of Nordic digital bank Lunar are launching a new company in Denmark called Repodo, targeting "the last major professional service the modern world forgot to rebuild", CEO Ken Villum Klausen said in revealing the venture on Monday.

Repodo provides an operating system for financial auditing that uses agentic AI to automate functions across data collection, reconciliation, documentation, and transaction analysis, with the final sign-off left to human judgement.

The company launches this week with €8.2 million in funding, described by Klausen as "one of the largest pre-seed rounds in Nordic history". London venture capital firm Hedosophia led the pre-seed round supported by Seed Capital. Both were early Lunar backers.

## Revolut launches euro-backed stablecoin EURR

UK challenger Revolut has launched EURR, an Ethereum-based, euro-denominated digital token, marking the first stage of its stablecoin strategy.

A phased rollout of EURR has commenced to eligible customers in Denmark, Poland, and Portugal, with "wider availability in other EEA markets expected later this year", the company says.

EURR is minted via Stripe's stablecoin infrastructure firm Bridge, before the token is integrated into Revolut's consumer retail app through Revolut Digital Assets Europe.

The current launch represents the inaugural phase of the bank's broader stablecoin strategy, with Revolut stating that additional currency-denominated stablecoins are being developed "through separate regulatory pathways".
