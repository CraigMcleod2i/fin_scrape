## Jeeves secures $110m Series C-1 funding for stablecoin platform expansion

US-based expense management software provider Jeeves has raised $110 million in new equity funding to scale its stablecoin business. CoinFund led the round with support from Coinbase Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz, and Y Combinator, among others.

CEO and founder Dileep Thazhmon confirmed the round on Tuesday via a statement posted to LinkedIn. He reveals that revenue has grown "more than 50% in a few months" since the Series C-1 term sheet was signed.

Thazhmon, who previously co-founded email marketing platform PowerInbox (now Jeeng), states that Jeeves will use the fresh funding to launch stablecoin wallets, which will support USDC and USDT, with payouts to 190 countries.

There will also be a new global AI spend tracking solution, accounts receivable (AR) module, and an expansion of the stablecoin corporate card to 10 new countries, primarily in South America.

## Nubank parent dismisses Monzo takeover speculation

Nu Holdings in Brazil has revealed a statement clarifying that "the company is not pursuing a transaction with Monzo", after media reports earlier this week alleged early discussions by the Nubank parent company to acquire the British challenger bank.

"Nu's capital allocation framework is unchanged," the group's statement continues. "Investment decisions are based on strategic fit, expected returns relative to the company's cost of capital, long-term value creation per share, and the management and financial resources required to execute the company's priorities."

The statement confirms that Nu Holdings is currently working to grow its business in Mexico, Colombia, and the US specifically. A UK drive is not mentioned.

## Valley National Bancorp pays $340m for SME banking platform Bluevine

Valley National Bancorp, the holding company of New Jersey-headquartered Valley National Bank, has agreed to acquire local fintech Bluevine for $340 million in a mix of cash and stock.

Expected to close in early 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions, the sale will be split 75% cash and 25% Valley common stock.

Founded in New Jersey in 2013, Bluevine has grown from a specialised invoice lender into a broader fintech offering checking and debit services to 175,000 small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs).

In addition to the transfer of deposits, roughly 180 employees from Bluevine's engineering, product, data science, and AI teams will also transition to the bank.

## Open banking fintech Trustly cuts 200 jobs globally

Trustly, the Stockholm-headquartered Pay by Bank technology provider, is downsizing its workforce with plans to cut around 200 roles from the organisation globally amid continued bottom line losses.

A company spokesperson confirmed the move to FinTech Futures: "These changes are about sharpening our focus and concentrating investment behind the priorities that will help us lead the rapidly growing open banking market."

According to LinkedIn, the company has a workforce of 865 people, meaning that this latest wave of job cuts will affect around a quarter of the business.

According to the group's annual report for the financial year ending 31 December 2025, Trustly's consolidated net loss widened by 33.2% to SEK 534.3 million ($54.4 million), up from a loss of SEK 401 million ($37.9 million) in 2024.

## Doku promotes co-founder Himelda Renuat to CEO amid Antom rebrand

Indonesian paytech Doku has named its co-founder and chief marketing officer Himelda Renuat as its new CEO, effective immediately. The appointment comes as the paytech deepens its partnership with Ant International's Antom and rebrands to Doku by Antom.

Doku signed an agreement with Antom and Ant International in February to scale Indonesian merchants' global payment processing capabilities. To showcase its synergy with Antom, alongside its regional payment processing subsidiary, 2C2P, Doku is rebranding to Doku by Antom.

A company statement notes that Doku will connect its "local payment methods, merchant relationships, and regulatory standing in Indonesia to Antom's global merchant acquiring platform and Ant International's broader product ecosystem, including the Alipay+ unified wallet gateway".
