## Fiserv promotes Takis Georgakopoulos to CEO as Mike Lyons joins Truist

US tech giant Fiserv has promoted Takis Georgakopoulos to CEO, effective immediately. Georgakopoulos was the global head of JP Morgan Payments for over 17 years before joining Fiserv as executive vice president in September 2024.

He takes over from Mike Lyons, who has led Fiserv as president and CEO-elect since January last year, but is now set to return to commercial banking as the new CEO of Truist Financial Corporation, effective 1 September.

The company, headquartered in Brookfield, Wisconsin, also said it is "reaffirming its outlook for the full year 2026" via its new CEO announcement this week, projecting organic revenue growth of 1% to 3% and adjusted earnings per share of $8.00 to $8.30.

## German government rejects UniCredit's share-exchange offer for Commerzbank

The German government has officially rejected UniCredit's share-exchange offer for Commerzbank.

UniCredit has been working to increase its stake in Commerzbank since acquiring an initial 9% stake from the German government in September 2024. Its stake had grown to around 30% by early 2026.

The government rejection regards a voluntary exchange offer made by UniCredit in May this year to swap its own shares for shares in Commerzbank and increase its holding even further.

The offer valued Commerzbank between €35 billion and €39 billion, and proposed an exchange of 0.485 UniCredit shares for each Commerzbank share.

The board at Commerzbank was quick to reject the deal, stating that UniCredit "has not presented a coherent and credible strategic plan for a combination", and advised shareholders to "not accept UniCredit’s exchange offer".

## Canada's Nuvei buys Payoneer for $2.75bn

Canada-based payments processor Nuvei has acquired US paytech Payoneer, a week after reports suggested a deal was close.

The deal will see Nuvei acquire "all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock of Payoneer Global Inc. for $7.40 per share in cash", according to a joint statement, with a total transaction equity value of around $2.75 billion.

Upon completion of the acquisition, anticipated for mid-2027, Nuvei will integrate all of Payoneer's capabilities, as well as its banking network, into its payment acceptance framework.

## Robinhood to cut full-time workforce by 10%

Robinhood is set to cut around 10% of its full-time workforce in an effort to "maintain a high performance culture, further accelerate product velocity, and remain lean and disciplined", according to a Form 8-K filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

The digital trading and investment platform, headquartered in Menlo Park, California, had around 2,900 full-time employees as of 31 December 2025, an earlier Form 10-K from February shows, with the workforce reduction therefore set to impact around 290 jobs.

In the statement announcing the job cuts, Vlad Tenev, the co-founder and long-time chairman and CEO of Robinhood, says that although Robinhood’s business "has never been stronger", to realise further scale and growth, he says "we cannot default to operating as a heavily-layered organisation".

## Flutterwave reaches $3.2bn valuation with Series E funding round

Flutterwave, a US-based paytech focused on emerging markets, has completed a Series E funding round at a company valuation of $3.2 billion.

While Flutterwave has not disclosed the exact amount secured in this latest round, the company confirmed that it has now raised over $500 million in funding to date. This includes a $250 million Series D round in 2022 that valued the firm at over $3 billion.

The Series E includes a notable strategic investment from Ripple, as Flutterwave continues to advance its stablecoin strategy.

Under the partnership, Ripple's USD-denominated stablecoin, RLUSD, will be integrated into Flutterwave's payment infrastructure and Send App remittance channels to settle high-volume transactions.
