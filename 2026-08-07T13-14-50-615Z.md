## Visa to acquire behavioural biometrics fintech BioCatch in $2.4bn deal

Visa has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Israeli fraud prevention fintech BioCatch for $2.4 billion in an all-cash deal.

The transaction, anticipated to close in Q2 2027, will see BioCatch's ownership transfer from funds advised by London-based VC Permira alongside shareholders including Bain Capital Tech Opportunities, CreditEase, and Maverick Ventures.

BioCatch will be incorporated into Visa's value-added services division, with the company's entire leadership team, including CEO Gadi Mazor, to continue in their current roles.

On the deal, Andrew Torre, president of value-added services at Visa, states that BioCatch's tech "will help our clients stop fraud before it reaches the point of payment".

## TMX Group to invest $800m in MEMX, combine the company with BOX

Canadian exchange operator TMX Group is set to combine US stock exchange MEMX and US options exchange BOX in a transaction that values the combined business at around $2.3 billion.

TMX, which owns and operates the Toronto Stock Exchange and the Montreal Stock Exchange, is investing approximately $800 million in equity to the merger as well as contributing its existing 53.2% ownership stake in BOX, acquired in 2008.

TMX will own around 59% of the combined business once the deal closes, expected in the second half of 2027, subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions.

## UBS hit with $125m fine by US FinCEN

UBS Financial Services (UBSFS), the US subsidiary of Swiss investment bank UBS, has been fined $125 million by the US Department of the Treasury’s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) for alleged "willful violations of the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA)", according to a statement from the regulator.

FinCEN says the fine represents "the largest penalty ever imposed against a broker-dealer for BSA violations to date". It arrives nearly eight years after UBSFS entered into a consent order with FinCEN addressing alleged violations of the BSA, which resulted in a civil money penalty of $14.5 million at the time.

In comments sent to FinTech Futures, a UBS spokesperson says that the announcement "brings closure to this legacy matter". The spokesperson adds: "UBS has cooperated fully with its regulators and has made significant investments to remediate and strengthen its AML programme in line with leading industry practices."

## Rabobank commits €2bn to three-year AI investment strategy

Rabobank in the Netherlands plans to commit up to €2 billion over the next three years to build out and scale its AI capabilities, according to an Interim Results 2026 report published this week.

Commenting on this commitment, Stefaan Decraene, who has led the bank as CEO since January 2023, says that AI, along with "other new technologies", will "further transform the way we work".

"To take full advantage of these developments, and most of all to meet evolving customer expectations, we will invest up to €2 billion in strengthening our data and IT foundation, enhancing customer experience and scaling AI," his statement reads.

## Former Stripe exec launches Increase Bank in US

US fintech Increase is launching a new FDIC-insured financial institution called Increase Bank. The entity combines regulated banking services with the financial infrastructure platform Increase has been building under founder Darragh Buckley since 2020.

CEO Buckley created Increase after spending six years working for Stripe, where he developed banking and payments infrastructure as part of the payment giant's early founding team.

Buckley's efforts to build a bank were made possible by the stake he acquired last year in Twin City Bank, a $114 million-asset community bank based in Longview, Washington.
