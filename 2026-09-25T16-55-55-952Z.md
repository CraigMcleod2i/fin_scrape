## Monzo EU CEO Michael Carney to step down

Monzo's EU CEO Michael Carney has announced he will be stepping down after two years in the role to launch his own AI accessibility venture.

Carney has led Monzo's European rollout, including securing a full European banking licence and commencing operations in Ireland, since joining the challenger bank from Stripe in 2024.

Carney, whose career also includes senior roles at Google and Twitter (now X), is leaving the challenger to found what he describes as a "personal passion": a start-up that will leverage AI and robotics to support people living with disabilities.

## Mobile money platform Airtel Money announces plans for major London IPO

Airtel Money, the mobile money and digital finance business of telecommunications giant Airtel Africa, has confirmed plans to pursue an initial public offering (IPO) on the London Stock Exchange (LSE).

The listing's final offer price will be "determined following publication of the Prospectus, subject to the completion of a book-building process, and is currently expected to be announced in mid October 2026", says Airtel Money.

Airtel Africa, majority-owned by Indian conglomerate Bharti Airtel, holds 77.85% of Airtel Money's issued ordinary share capital. In 2021, TPG, Mastercard, the Qatar Investment Authority, and Chimetech Holding Ltd acquired minority stakes in the fintech unit for a combined $550 million.

## Stablecoin provider Bastion lands conditional OCC trust bank approval

Stablecoin infrastructure provider Bastion has received preliminary conditional approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) for a national trust bank charter in the US.

The charter will enable Bastion to establish a new entity, Bastion Platforms National Trust Company, which will deliver "fiduciary custody of USDC and other GENIUS Act-compliant digital assets, white-label stablecoin wallet and issuance services for enterprise clients — including minting, redemption and conversion between stablecoins and fiat", explains the fintech.

This represents the latest step in Bastion's journey toward federal supervision following its acquisition of a New York trust charter in February 2025. The company has not provided a timeline for when it expects to receive full licence approval.

## Adyen names Klarna CFO Niclas Neglen as next finance chief

Dutch payments technology company Adyen has nominated Klarna's Niclas Neglen as its next chief financial officer (CFO), effective 1 February 2027, subject to regulatory and shareholder approval.

Neglen, who will also join Adyen's statutory management board, currently works as CFO at buy now, pay later giant Klarna, a position he has held since 2021.

Neglen will replace interim CFO Hwa Tsao, who assumed responsibility for Adyen's financial strategy in August following Ethan Tandowsky's departure to ElevenLabs.

Once Neglen is permanently in position, Tsao will resume oversight of Adyen's finance operations as senior vice president of group finance.

## UK banks complete first live retail trials using tokenised deposits

A number of major high street banks have executed the first set of live retail transactions in the UK using tokenised sterling deposits. Participants included NatWest, Lloyds Banking Group, HSBC UK, Barclays, Nationwide Building Society, Santander, and challenger bank Monzo, which were convened by trade body UK Finance under the Great British Tokenised Deposit (GBTD) initiative.

The pilot involved three separate transaction trials. Two of these were remortgage completions, while the third was a basic peer-to-peer transaction, in which a consumer bought an item from a private seller in a typical online marketplace setting.

The banks used the GBTD platform for the pilot. The platform was developed by Quant, a blockchain and distributed ledger technology infrastructure provider first selected by UK Finance for the GBTD initiative in September 2025.

UK Finance states that further pilots using the GBTD platform are expected "over the next few months", and will look to test digital asset settlements by linking tokenised deposits with digital assets.
