## TabaPay raises $155m and confirms Transact Bank takeover bid

US-based payments processor TabaPay has raised $155 million in a strategic growth financing round led by first-time investor FTV Capital, and has now also revealed plans to acquire Transact Bank NA in Colorado.

This is the first institutional investment figure to be revealed by TabaPay, following a SoftBank Vision Fund 2 investment in 2022. The round has brought FTV Capital partner Robert Anderson onto the TabaPay board of directors.

The money is being used directly to fund the acquisition of Transact Bank, which is a federally chartered and FDIC-insured bank operating out of Denver. TabaPay says that once the acquisition is closed, expected in Q4 2026, subject to regulatory approval, Transact Bank will be rebranded as TabaBank NA.

TabaBank will be structured to "complement TabaPay’s existing partner bank network", according to the purchase announcement, providing "additional redundancy and expertise for challenging use cases".

## OpenPayd gears up for Nasdaq listing with MSB USA integration

Serial fintech founder Dr Ozan Ozerk is merging two of his portfolio companies, London-based OpenPayd and MSB USA in Texas, ahead of a planned $1.145 billion SPAC merger and Nasdaq listing expected later this year.

The move will see MSB USA, which provides fintechs and corporate clients with US payment routing, USD accounts, and cross-border money transfer services, integrated into OpenPayd, a Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) and embedded finance platform.

The move will bring to the OpenPayd group the 43 state money transmitter licenses held by MSB USA, providing "increased geographic reach for OpenPayd’s global clients operating in or expanding into the US", according to a company statement.

OpenPayd has been seeking to list in the US since June, when it revealed plans to merge with special purpose acquisition company Titan Acquisition Corp.

## Ingenico builds out leadership team with new tech, product and revenue chiefs

French paytech Ingenico has made a plethora of new appointments, onboarding a new chief technology officer (CTO), chief product officer (CPO), chief revenue officer (CRO), and chief customer excellence officer (CXO). All four hires are effective immediately.

To lead the company's tech division, Ingenico has appointed seasoned executive Paul Gardiner as CTO, succeeding Patrick Blanc. Maria Parpou joins from Mastercard as CPO, taking over from Erik Vlugt, who departed Ingenico in July after three years in the role.

Meanwhile, the new CRO is Ieuan Owen, bringing experience Xplor, Monitor Deloitte, Royal Mail and Worldpay, while Colt Technology Services veteran Oliver Moore joins as CXO.

Last month, Ingenico secured a €150 million investment to reset its capital structure and serve as the foundation to propel the company into its next phase of growth. At the time, Ingenico said one of the key areas of development in this next phase was growing its leadership team.

## Remittance platform Félix bags $200m to fuel AI-powered product expansion

US-based cross-border paytech Félix has scored $200 million in a mix of equity and debt as the company looks to "expand beyond remittances into AI-powered financial services".

The investment includes an Andreessen Horowitz-led $87 million equity raise that also saw participation from QED Investors, Castle Island Ventures, Switch Ventures, Contour Venture Partners, and Endeavor Catalyst. Additionally, Félix also secured a $113 million credit line commitment from General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund.

With the new funding, the firm plans to expand its product suite, highlighting lending and savings as areas of interest. Félix is also looking to broaden its use of AI in the platform, building out its product offering around its Cognitive Financial Companion.

## TD Bank completes tokenised payment trials under Project Agorá

TD Bank Group in Canada has completed its first live trial of tokenised asset settlement using the shared blockchain-based ledger created as part of Project Agorá. The domestic test routed live US dollar funds between TD Bank NA and TD New York Branch (TD Securities LLC), using Bank of New York as the clearing intermediary.

TD states that the trial "demonstrated the ability to issue tokenised money on the Agorá platform and facilitate the instant movement of money, with atomic settlement, between the two TD entities".

The latest trial under Project Agorá builds upon TD’s previous work with tokenisation. Earlier this year, TD Securities served as a joint lead manager for Project Samara, a collaborative pilot with the Bank of Canada, Royal Bank of Canada, and Export Development Canada.
